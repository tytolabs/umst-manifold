## 1. Literature: staggered vs monolithic damage–mechanics

### 1.1 Phase-field damage and variational structure

**AT1 / AT2 phase-field models** regularize brittle fracture with a smooth damage field \(d\) and a length scale \(l\); the tensile elastic energy is degraded by \(g(d)\) while a fracture term penalizes gradients of \(d\) and enforces dissipation tied to \(G_c\). **Miehe, Hofacker, Welschinger** (*Comput. Methods Appl. Mech. Engrg.*, 2010, “A phase field model for rate-independent crack propagation”) give a **thermodynamically consistent** incremental formulation with **history-type irreversibility** for the tensile driving variable—standard reference for spectral elastic split + damage evolution in quasi-static brittle fracture.

**Bourdin–Francfort–Marigo (BFM)** (*J. Elasticity*, 2000; related numerical treatments) motivate the **variational fracture** viewpoint: global energy minimization / \(\Gamma\)-convergence links the regularized functional to sharp cracks as \(l \to 0\) with mesh refinement—relevant to the repo’s **multi-\((l_0,h)\)** deferral, not yet a CI harness.

### 1.2 Staggered (operator-split) vs monolithic

- **Staggered / alternate minimization:** At each load or pseudo-time increment, iterate **(i)** equilibrium at **fixed** damage \(d^k\) (elastic subproblem with degraded stiffness \(g(d^k)\)), then **(ii)** damage update at **fixed** strain \(\varepsilon^{k+1}\) from the displacement field. This is an **operator split**. It is attractive because each sub-step reuses **black-box** elasticity and a **local or graph-smoothed** damage solver (as in the shipped AT2 relaxation).

- **Monolithic:** Solve for \((u,d)\) jointly (Newton or alternate direction with consistent tangents). Offers better stability in highly coupled regimes but requires **coupled Jacobians** or sophisticated line search—**out of scope** for the current `PhaseFieldFractureSolver` (inner relaxation only).

### 1.3 Operator-split stability (sketch)

Staggered schemes can **lag** the coupling between elastic energy and damage, which may manifest as **energy drift** or **mesh-dependent** dissipation unless outer iterations or small increments are used. Practical mitigations used in the literature and in engineering codes include:

- **Multiple staggered passes per increment** (outer \(k\) loop) until a **coupled residual** or **energy stationarity** test passes.
- **Adaptive stepping** in load or pseudo-time when the damage increment is large.
- **Implicit** treatment of the **most stiff** component (often displacement) with an **explicit** damage map, or vice versa, depending on the regime—here elasticity is quasi-static linear in \(u\) at fixed \(d\), damage is nonlinear but local/smoothed on the graph.

The shipped code does **not** yet certify energy monotone bands across \((l_0,h)\); that matches **DEFERRAL — Fracture** (“energy monotone band on benchmark chain”).

## 2. Current code hooks

### 2.1 `PhaseFieldFractureSolver` (`src/physics/solvers/fracture_field.rs`)

| Mechanism | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **`update_damage`** | Inner AT2-style relaxation at **fixed** \(\varepsilon\): spectral tensile \(\psi^+\) via cyclic Jacobi on \(3\times3\) blocks, graph Laplacian on \(d\), red–black under-relaxation, clamp \([0,1]\), then **`max(d_{\mathrm{old}}, d_{\mathrm{trial}})`** for irreversibility. |
| **`update_damage_staggered`** | **Outer** loop only: for each `outer_iterations`, sets `strain = strain_fn(&damage)`, then `damage ← update_damage(strain, damage, …)`. Does **not** call mechanics; the strain provider is the **orchestrator hook**. |
| **`strain_tensor_from_bar_network_displacement`** | Builds symmetric \(\varepsilon(\mathbf u)\) `[B,N,3,3]` from bar-network kinematics (same convention as **`strain_tensor_for_fracture_after_mechanics`** after one `solve_equilibrium`). Used by **`thmc-coupled`** on the SI `[N,3]` path and by the packaged `strain_tensor_for_fracture_after_mechanics` helper (`fracture_field.rs`). |
| **`g(d)`** | Documented as \((1-d)^2 + \eta\) for future tight coupling; **not** used inside `update_damage` (strain-only drive for \(d\)). |
| **BFM / Γ** | Sharp-interface Γ-limit across meshes and full energy monotone bands are **deferred** (see status doc); inner admissibility and partial multi-scale checks live in the harness below. |
| **`tests/verification/fracture_gamma_convergence.rs`** | **`#[test]`** names aligned with **`docs/Solver-Status.md`**: `at2_length_scale_sweep_non_regression`, `at2_gc_linear_scaling_smoke`, `at2_gamma_convergence_three_length_scales` (three \((l_0,h)\) pairs, \(h/l_0=\tfrac14\), fixed-strain relaxation — partial Γ-type band vs **`Gc`**), **`at2_discrete_surface_functional_toy_chain_matches_hand_total`** (hand-checked 3-node \(D_h\) regression on the same discrete functional), `at2_gamma_convergence_multi_ratio_schedule_smoke`, **`at2_gamma_convergence_multi_ratio_psi_plus_schedule_smoke`**, `staggered_fracture_compliance_monotone_increasing`, `staggered_two_outer_strains_exceeds_single_pass_weak_strain_only`, `at2_surface_energy_scale_matches_gc_order_of_magnitude`. |

### 2.2 `VectorMechanicsSolver` (`src/physics/mechanics.rs`)

| Mechanism | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **`packed_bar_network_equilibrium`** | Assembles axial \(k_e \propto (EA/L)\cdot\bigl((1-d_{\mathrm{edge}})^2 + \text{DAMAGE\_REG}\bigr)\) with \(d\) averaged to edges; **projected CG** on the Dirichlet subspace (`boundary_mask`). Optional **Jacobi diagonal preconditioner** (`use_preconditioner`). |
| **`solve_equilibrium`** | Public entry: returns \((u,\sigma)\) with **bar-network** rank-one nodal stress. |
| **`voigt_strain_from_edge_displacement`** | Nodal Voigt \(\varepsilon\) `[B,N,6]` from edge data \((\mathbf u_{\mathrm{tgt}}-\mathbf u_{\mathrm{src}})\cdot\hat{\mathbf t}\), rank-one spread, degree-normalized sum—natural bridge from \(u\) to **symmetric tensor** \(\varepsilon\) for fracture. |
| **Q1 hex / PCG** | **Separate** path: `q1_hex_elasticity::hex_solve_pcg_masked` and `ExtrudedPlateMechanics` (plate / brick scaffold). Not invoked by `PhaseFieldFractureSolver`; future staggered coupling could swap the **elasticity kernel** while keeping the same **`strain_fn`** contract. |

Degradation in mechanics matches the **square** \((1-d)^2\) spirit (plus `DAMAGE_REG`), consistent with the documented \(g(d)\) in fracture docs.

### 2.3 THMC strain wiring (`src/physics/solvers/thmc.rs`)

Inside `ThmcSolver::step` (`thmc-coupled`, which **pulls** `fracture-at2`):

1. **Mechanics:** If `manifold.node_positions` is `[N,3]` and BC mask shapes align, **`VectorMechanicsSolver::solve_equilibrium`** runs with **`damage_m`** (first damage channel, fixed at **step entry** for the outer-pass loop) and updates `state.mechanical.displacement`.
2. **Fracture (after outer passes):** If the same `[N,3]` embedding is present, strain fed to **`PhaseFieldFractureSolver::update_damage`** is **`strain_tensor_from_bar_network_displacement`** on the **final** `state.mechanical.displacement` and `node_positions` — i.e. **post-mechanics** \(\varepsilon(\mathbf u)\) on the SI bar path (same Voigt packing as **`strain_tensor_for_fracture_after_mechanics`** after one solve; see **`tests/verification/thmc_drying_shrinkage.rs`** — `bar_network_strain_matches_strain_tensor_for_fracture_after_mechanics`). If positions are missing, not `[N,3]`, or the mask blocks the equilibrium branch, strain falls back to **`strain_tensor_from_manifold`** (`matrix_features[..,0,..]` or zeros) — see `thmc` module rustdoc and **`docs/Solver-Status.md`** (**DEFERRAL — Fracture**).

**Still a research gap vs full staggered theory:** mechanics and transport still see **`damage_m` from step start** for every outer pass; fracture runs **once** after the loop — there is no **within-step** alternation \(u^{(k)}\!\to\!d^{(k)}\!\to\!u^{(k+1)}\) inside `max_newton`. Post-mechanics **`strain_tensor_from_bar_network_displacement`** closes the \(\varepsilon(\mathbf u)\) feed on the SI bar path; iterating \(u\) with an updated damage field inside the same THMC step remains **out of scope** for the current `ThmcSolver::step` wiring.

## 3. Blueprint: outer-loop API

### 3.1 Target physics (one quasi-static increment)

Given body force \(\mathbf f\), boundary data, and material parameters:

1. Initialize \(d^{(0)}\) (e.g. previous increment).
2. **For** \(k = 0,1,\ldots,K-1\) **or until convergence:**

   **Outer A — Elasticity at fixed damage:**  
   Solve for \(\mathbf u^{(k+1)}\) such that equilibrium holds with stiffness degraded by \(g(d^{(k)})\), using the shipped **bar network** (today) or a **hex continuum** kernel later. Recover **nodal** \(\varepsilon^{(k+1)} = \varepsilon(\mathbf u^{(k+1)})\) (e.g. Voigt map → symmetric \(3\times3\)).

   **Outer B — Damage at fixed strain:**  
   \(d^{(k+1)} = \texttt{update\_damage}\bigl(\varepsilon^{(k+1)},\, d^{(k)},\,G_c,\,l,\,\texttt{edges\_b1}\bigr)\).

3. **Irreversibility** is already enforced **inside** `update_damage` via `max(d_old, d_trial)` after inner relaxation.

### 3.2 Call-site shape (already partially available)

- **`update_damage_staggered(strain_fn, damage0, gc, edges, outer_iterations)`** where `strain_fn(&d)` **must** implement: “solve mechanics with **this** \(d\)” → build \(\varepsilon(\mathbf u)\) → return `[B,N,3,3]` for fracture.

Today **`strain_fn` is generic**; packaged helpers include **`strain_tensor_for_fracture_after_mechanics`** (one equilibrium + strain tensor) and, on the **`thmc-coupled`** SI **`[N,3]`** path, **`ThmcSolver::step`** uses **`strain_tensor_from_bar_network_displacement`** on the equilibrium **`u`** (same kinematics as the helper after one solve — `bar_network_strain_matches_strain_tensor_for_fracture_after_mechanics`). A **single authoritative** “mechanics with arbitrary `d` inside `strain_fn` + THMC state writeback” hook for full staggered outer loops in THMC remains optional API sugar; the tensor pipeline itself is in place.

### 3.3 Termination criteria (recommended)

| Criterion | Formula / action |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Displacement–damage residual** | \(\|\mathbf u^{(k+1)}-\mathbf u^{(k)}\|_\infty\) and \(\|d^{(k+1)}-d^{(k)}\|_\infty\) below `tol_u`, `tol_d`. |
| **Energy band** | Compare discrete mechanical work + regularized fracture functional increment to a **monotone band** (deferral acceptance). |
| **Cap** | `max_outer` to avoid infinite splits when softening is strong. |

Host-side scalar reads for early exit are **acceptable in orchestration / tests** even when inner kernels stay device-tensor-pure (THMC already documents tradeoffs for Newton early exit).

## 7. Research backlog (Gamma-limit, psi-plus, multi-(l0,h) schedules)

**Scope:** everything below is **not** default-CI today; it sharpens **DEFERRAL — Fracture** (“Γ-limit with driven \(\psi^+\)”, “broader \((l_0,h)\) schedules”, “dissipation vs \(G_c\) on refined meshes under staggered \(u\!\leftrightarrow\!d\)”) into **named** acceptance objects and **`#[test]`** targets. The shipped partial check remains **`at2_gamma_convergence_three_length_scales`** (\(\psi^+\!=0\) exponential seed, fixed \(h/l_0=\tfrac14\), three pairs).

### 7.1 Discrete surface functional (1-D bar, already in harness)

For nodal damage \(d_i\) on a uniform chain with spacing \(h\), length scale \(\ell_0\), and critical energy \(G_c\) (constant per node in the harness),

\[
D_h \;=\; G_c \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} \frac{d_i^2\,h}{2\ell_0}
    \;+\; G_c \sum_{i=0}^{N-2} \frac{\ell_0}{2}\left(\frac{d_{i+1}-d_i}{h}\right)^2 h \,.
\]

**Regression guard:** `tests/verification/fracture_gamma_convergence.rs` — **`at2_discrete_surface_functional_toy_chain_matches_hand_total`** (closed-form check on a 3-node toy vector). **`at2_gamma_convergence_three_length_scales`** uses the same definition via **`discrete_at2_bar_surface_energy_1d`**.

### 7.2 Gamma-type band with **nonzero** tensile driving \(\psi^+\)

**Setup:** same 1-D bar graph and \((\ell_0,h)\) refinement family as **`at2_gamma_convergence_three_length_scales`**, but enforce **nonzero** spectral tensile \(\psi^+\) from the strain tensor the solver actually uses (e.g. uniform \(\varepsilon_{xx}>0\) large enough that **`update_damage`** perturbs \(d\) away from the pure exponential seed, or a documented load ramp / outer strain schedule).

**Acceptance (proposal):**

1. **Band vs \(G_c\):** \(\displaystyle \frac{|D_h - G_c|}{G_c} < \tau_\Gamma\) with \(\tau_\Gamma\) chosen from a baseline run (the current \(\psi^+\!=0\) gate uses **2%**; a coupled \(\psi^+\) case may need a **wider** \(\tau_\Gamma\) until mechanics is in the loop).
2. **Non-worsening across refinements:** with successive relative errors \(\varepsilon_k\) at the same three \((\ell_0,h)\) pairs as today, \(\varepsilon_{k+1} \le \varepsilon_k + 10^{-3}\) (same slack pattern as **`at2_gamma_convergence_three_length_scales`**).
3. **Drive sanity:** \(\displaystyle \max_i \psi^+_i \ge \delta\) for a documented \(\delta>0\) (values taken from the same spectral split **`update_damage`** applies).

**Proposed `#[test]` name:** **`at2_gamma_convergence_psi_plus_nonzero_three_length_scales`** (feature `fracture-at2`).

### 7.3 Multi-ratio \((\ell_0,h)\) schedules

**Setup:** fix bar length \(L\) and ratios \(\rho_j = h_j/\ell_{0,j}\) from a small set (e.g. \(\{\tfrac18,\tfrac14,\tfrac12\}\)); for each \(j\), pick consistent \(h_j,\ell_{0,j}\) and run the same fixed-strain or staggered harness.

**Acceptance (proposal):** for each \(j\), \(\displaystyle \frac{|D_{h_j} - G_c|}{G_c} < \tau_j\) with documented \(\tau_j\) (may depend on \(\rho_j\)); optional golden non-regression of \((D_{h_j},\max d)\).

**Shipped `#[test]` name:** **`at2_gamma_convergence_multi_ratio_schedule_smoke`**. Optional CSV non-regression: **`at2_gamma_convergence_multi_ratio_non_regression_csv`**.

### 7.3.1 Multi-ratio schedule with nonzero tensile \(\psi^+\)

**Setup:** identical \(\rho_j=h_j/\ell_{0,j}\) rows as §7.3 (fixed \(\ell_0\), \(\rho\in\{\tfrac18,\tfrac14,\tfrac12\}\)) with the same exponential damage seed and 32-pass fixed-strain relaxation, but impose **uniform** \(\varepsilon_{xx}>0\) so **`spectral_tensile_psi_plus_from_strain`** matches the solver’s tensile drive.

**Acceptance (shipped smoke):** per row \(j\), documented \(\tau_{\Gamma,j}\) on \(\displaystyle |D_{h_j}-G_c|/G_c\) (wider than the \(\psi^+\!=0\) §7.3 \(\tau_j\) caps — coupled mechanics-free drive); **`max_i \psi^+_i \ge \delta\)** with the same \(\delta\) pattern as §7.2; and \(D_{h_j} > G_c + 10^{-3}\) on each mesh (finest \(\rho\) rows can sit barely above the sharp surface-only optimum).

**Shipped `#[test]` name:** **`at2_gamma_convergence_multi_ratio_psi_plus_schedule_smoke`** (feature `fracture-at2`).

### 7.3.2 Multi-ratio schedule with **outer** tensile strain ramp

**Setup:** identical \(\rho_j=h_j/\ell_{0,j}\) rows, exponential damage seed, and terminal \(\varepsilon_{xx}\) as §7.3.1, but **`PhaseFieldFractureSolver::update_damage_staggered`** applies a **documented linear ramp** of \(\varepsilon_{xx}\) across 32 outer passes (memo §7.2 “load ramp / outer strain schedule”), rather than holding strain fixed for 32 repeated **`update_damage`** calls.

**Shipped `#[test]` name:** **`at2_gamma_convergence_multi_ratio_psi_plus_outer_strain_ramp_smoke`** (feature `fracture-at2`).
